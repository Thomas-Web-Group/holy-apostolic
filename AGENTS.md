## Content policy

- For every quotation, identify author/issuing church, work, edition/translation, location, URL, and reproduction basis. Verify against the actual text. Preserve wording; mark omissions. Do not invent quotations, citations, translators, permissions, or bibliographic dates.
- Ancient authors' modern translations may be copyrighted. Substantial passages require verified public-domain editions or an applicable licence/permission. Otherwise use a permitted brief quotation and a clearly named full reading link.
- Required licence attributions must appear beside quotations and in source records. John Piper excerpts used under Desiring God's permission must carry the stated attribution and permission link; that permission does not cover every author on that site.
- The four supplied Mother of God Church rules retain their exact reference links. Native prayer texts are now explicitly authorized and must be complete verified published units with permission/public-domain provenance. Keep editions distinct; do not imply the 1982 library is the user's Mother of God rule. Preserve printed wording/order and source rubrics, including conditional clergy alternatives. No invented prayers or assembled rules.
- Keep Orthodox, PCA confessional, and Piper/Baptist sources clearly attributed. Do not add unquoted commentary that purports to resolve their disagreements.
